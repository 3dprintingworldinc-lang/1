FAQ

How can materials really be $0?

Our filament supply costs us nothing, so we pass that straight through. You pay for machine time, expertise, and finishing — the parts of the job that actually determine quality.

What if my print fails or arrives damaged?

We reprint it free, no questions. A reprint costs us only time, so our guarantee is the strongest in the industry.

STL, 3MF, OBJ, and STEP. No file? Send photos and measurements — our design service starts at $60/hr and most small parts take under an hour to model.

How do I pay?

You'll receive an itemized invoice by email with payment instructions once your quote is confirmed. Nothing is charged until you approve the final price.

Do you ship everywhere?

We ship anywhere your postal service reaches. Shipping is a flat $6 and free on orders over $60.
